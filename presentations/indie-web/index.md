# The Indie Web

### Why the internet still can be a fun whimsical place and how you can carve out your part in it!

---

While most of us tend to spend the majority of their time on the internet in curated walled-off gardens of social media, 
the internet of old is still very much alive and kicking & lot's of people are re-discovering it for the first time!

And to prove to you how easy it is to make your place on the internet, this whole presentation is actually part of my personal website!
